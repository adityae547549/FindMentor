Online Mentorship Platform for Indian School Students

FindMentor is built for school students in India who want more than recorded videos and crowded tuitions. If you are in Classes 6–12 and feel shy to ask questions in class, or you keep getting stuck on the same types of sums and theory questions, 1‑on‑1 mentoring can change how you learn.

Each session is tailored to your board syllabus. You and your mentor go topic by topic, revising what was taught in school, filling gaps from previous classes and practising questions that are likely to appear in exams. Instead of rushing through chapters, mentors keep checking whether you have actually understood the idea before moving ahead.

Who FindMentor Is For

FindMentor is ideal for students who want personalised help in Maths, Biology, Chemistry and English. Whether you are just entering middle school and need help with basics, or preparing for board exams and pre‑boards, mentors adjust speed and difficulty to your level. Parents who want regular progress updates and a clear plan for improvement also find mentoring helpful.

Subjects and Learning Style

Mentors cover textbook concepts, NCERT and reference books, diagrams, derivations, grammar and long‑answer writing. Sessions are interactive: students solve problems live, explain concepts back to the mentor, and get immediate feedback when they make mistakes. Homework and revision tasks are designed so that you are not overloaded, but still keep moving forward each week.

Why 1‑on‑1 Is Better Than Only Watching YouTube

YouTube is great for quick explanations, but it cannot watch you solve a question and point out exactly where your thinking goes wrong. In a 1‑on‑1 session, mentors see your rough work, hear how you approach a problem and correct misunderstandings early. Instead of passively watching ten videos, you actively practise fewer problems with deeper understanding.

Long‑Term Benefits

Consistent mentoring builds confidence, not just marks. Students learn how to plan their study time, how to break big chapters into smaller goals, and how to revise effectively before tests. These skills stay useful in higher classes, entrance exams and even college. FindMentor aims to be the quiet support system behind every student who wants to genuinely understand their subjects, not just memorise them.
