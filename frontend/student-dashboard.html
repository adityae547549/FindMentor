Student Dashboard Overview

The FindMentor student dashboard is the home base for Indian school students using 1‑on‑1 online mentoring. It helps you see upcoming sessions, recent study activity and quick actions like starting a video call or asking the built‑in study assistant.

When a student from Classes 6–12 logs in, the dashboard shows their next booked mentoring session, subjects they are actively working on, and a history of recent calls. Instead of hunting through messages or links, everything related to mentorship is organised in one place.

Key Features

  • • Quick access button to start a 1‑on‑1 mentoring session
  • • Snapshot of upcoming and past sessions with mentors
  • • Recent study assistant questions for fast revision
  • • Progress view to see which subjects you are focusing on
  • • Links to update profile, class and preferred subjects

Designed for Classes 6–12

The dashboard layout is simple enough for middle‑school students and powerful enough for board‑exam preparation. Subjects such as Maths, Biology, Chemistry and English are highlighted clearly, so students always know which topics they are currently working on with mentors.

Parents can sit with their child and quickly understand what has been covered recently, which topics are pending and when the next sessions are scheduled. This transparency helps build trust between families and mentors.

One Click to Join Sessions

From the dashboard, students can join ongoing mentoring sessions with a single click. The video call screen opens directly in the browser and connects them to the right mentor without searching for links in chats or emails.

If a student has a doubt while revising, they can quickly start a session or use the built‑in study assistant to get hints before the next class. This reduces friction and keeps learning continuous instead of waiting for the next tuition batch.

Track Learning Progress

The student dashboard surfaces high‑level progress indicators such as how many sessions were completed this week, how many questions were asked to the study assistant and which subjects got the most attention.

Over time, this gives students and parents a clear picture of whether the mentoring plan is being followed and which areas may need extra focus before exams.

Secure, Private Access

Only logged‑in students can see their own dashboard. Each learner’s sessions, activity and personal information are protected behind Firebase Authentication and are not visible to search engines.

This public page simply explains how the student dashboard works. When you sign up and log in, you will see your own personalised version based on your subjects and mentor sessions.
