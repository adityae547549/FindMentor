Our Subject Mentors

Every mentor on FindMentor works with Indian school students and focuses on board‑aligned exam preparation for Classes 6–12.

Ankush Kumar

Mathematics Mentor – Classes 8–12

M.Sc Mathematics • 6+ Years Experience

Ankush specialises in helping students who feel that Maths is their “weak subject”. He has taught Algebra, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry and Calculus to hundreds of school students across CBSE and State boards. Instead of jumping straight into formulas, he starts by revising pre‑requisite concepts from earlier classes so that students never feel lost.

During 1‑on‑1 sessions, Ankush asks students to solve questions out loud and explain their thinking. This makes it easy to spot exactly where a mistake happens – whether it is in understanding the question, setting up the equation or doing the calculation. He then shows alternative methods, short tricks for mental checks, and gives a small set of targeted homework questions to build speed and accuracy.

If you keep making “silly mistakes”, feel nervous about word problems or want to strengthen your base before boards, Ankush’s mentoring offers a calm, patient environment to rebuild confidence in Maths step by step.

Ankit Kumar

English & Social Studies Mentor – Classes 6–10

M.A. English • 8+ Years Experience

Ankit focuses on students who struggle with writing answers that actually score marks. In English and Social Studies, many students know the story or concept but lose marks because their answers are either too short, too long or not structured as examiners expect. Ankit breaks chapters into simple narratives, highlights important lines from textbooks and shows how to convert understanding into high‑quality written answers.

His sessions include live answer‑writing practice where students first attempt a question on their own and then compare it to an ideal answer. Ankit points out what to keep, what to remove and how to improve grammar, vocabulary and presentation. Over time, students develop their own templates for letter‑writing, comprehension, long answers and map‑based questions.

If English pulls down your overall percentage or you want to build strong communication skills early, Ankit’s mentoring will help you express your thoughts clearly and confidently in exams and beyond.

Shubham Singh

Biology Mentor – Classes 9–12

Ph.D Biology • 7+ Years Experience

Shubham knows that Biology can feel like “too much theory” for many students. Long chapters, heavy terminology and detailed diagrams make it easy to get overwhelmed close to exams. His teaching style converts dense information into visual stories. He uses diagrams, everyday examples and simple analogies so that students can picture each process clearly in their mind.

In 1‑on‑1 sessions, Shubham spends time on how to memorise and recall information efficiently. He shares spaced‑repetition notes, quick revision sheets and tips on how to draw labelled diagrams fast under time pressure. Students learn how to prioritise which lines to underline in the textbook and how to approach 3‑mark and 5‑mark questions without leaving important points.

If you keep forgetting definitions or mixing up similar concepts in Botany and Zoology, Shubham’s mentoring will help you convert Biology from a fear subject into a scoring subject.

Yash Kumar

Chemistry Mentor – Classes 9–12

M.Sc Chemistry • 10+ Years Experience

Yash helps students who feel lost between Physical, Organic and Inorganic Chemistry. He believes that once the core ideas behind moles, balancing equations and reaction mechanisms are clear, most problems become straightforward. His sessions alternate between concept building, numerical practice and quick oral quizzes to keep students engaged.

For board‑exam students, Yash focuses strongly on previous‑year questions and common mistake patterns. He shows how to read questions carefully, identify the right formula or reaction, and avoid unit conversion errors. Students also learn simple memory techniques to remember reaction series and periodic trends without rote learning entire tables.

If you want Chemistry to stop pulling down your Science grade or you plan to take Science in higher classes, Yash’s guidance will give you a solid, exam‑ready foundation.
