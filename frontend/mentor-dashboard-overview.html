Mentor Dashboard Overview

The FindMentor mentor dashboard is where teachers and subject experts organise their 1‑on‑1 sessions with Indian school students. It brings together booking requests, upcoming calls and past sessions in one place so mentors can focus on teaching instead of admin work.

When a mentor logs in, they immediately see which students are waiting for help, which sessions are scheduled next and how their weekly teaching hours look. This makes it easy to plan classes around other commitments and avoid double‑booking.

Key Mentor Dashboard Features

  • • View incoming student help requests in real time
  • • Approve or schedule new 1‑on‑1 sessions
  • • One‑click join button for live video calls
  • • Quick access to chat and study assistant activity
  • • Overview of weekly teaching load and active students

Built for Subject Mentors

The mentor dashboard is designed for teachers who handle school subjects such as Maths, Biology, Chemistry and English for Classes 6–12. It groups sessions by subject and student so mentors can quickly remember what was taught last time and what should be covered next.

Instead of keeping notes scattered across chat apps, mentors can see their current students, active topics and time spent per subject directly inside the dashboard.

Smooth Video Call Experience

From the mentor dashboard, teachers can join live sessions with one click. The system uses WebRTC and a secure signalling backend so mentors and students can share audio and video reliably, even on average home internet connections.

Mentors can manage multiple sessions in a day without juggling separate meeting links. Each scheduled class automatically appears with a “Join Session” action.

Stay on Top of Student Requests

When students press the “Request Help” button from their dashboard, those requests appear in the mentor dashboard. Mentors can accept, reschedule or decline based on availability, keeping expectations clear for students and parents.

This flow works especially well for school evenings and weekends, when many students need last‑minute help with homework or tests.

Private Workspace for Mentors

Only authenticated mentors can access the full mentor dashboard. Student details, notes and session history are private and are not indexed by search engines.

This public overview page is meant to show potential mentors how the system works before they sign up. After registration and approval, they receive their own secure dashboard view.
